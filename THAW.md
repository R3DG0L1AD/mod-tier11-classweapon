## The quest (about 1-2 hours)
1. **A Crown That Hums**: talk to Tirion's Echo at the Guild House.
2. **Escape the Frozen King**: run Halls of Reflection (Normal or Heroic). The Captain's Chest at the end has a Shard of the Thawed Crown for every real player on the quest. Tirion's Echo can teleport you to the entrance.
3. **Class Trial**: ask Tirion's Echo to call your class's trial boss (for example Old Mossback for hunters) and beat it. If you lose, you're brought back up and can try again. Win to get a Token of the Thaw Trial.
4. **The Forging**: right-click the Black Sun to open the Vril Forge, choose "Forge my legendary", enjoy the fireworks, then tell Tirion's Echo. If a weapon is ever lost, the Forge offers to make it again.
